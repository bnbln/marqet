The raster badges are 512 px square to render crisply in the card header, store grid, and list rows. Other existing marks retain their bundled sources.

## Added 28.09.2026

All marks sit behind the brand-logo switch (`MARQET_BRAND_LOGOS = NO` → monograms, `StoreBadge.usesBrandLogos`). They are laid out for the round badge: either the retailer's own square web/app icon, or the vector wordmark centred on white so that it fits inside the circle (512 px). V-Markt has no usable source and keeps its monogram.
