# Marqet Privacy Policy

*Draft, 28 Sep 2026: replace placeholders and have it reviewed before publishing.*

**Controller:** [Name / Company], [Address], Germany · [support@…]

**In short:** Marqet has no account, no server of its own, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We do not receive any personal data from you.

**Processed on your device only**
- **Reminders:** With your permission, Marqet reads the list you choose in Apple Reminders and writes an item's store as a line "🛒 Store" into its notes. This data lives in Apple Reminders / your iCloud account and is synced by Apple. People you share the list with can see this line.
- **Your stores, branches, settings and learned data** stay on your device.
- **Location (optional):** used to find branches nearby and sort stores by distance, via Apple Maps (MapKit). Marqet neither stores nor transmits your location to us.
- **Loyalty cards (optional):** name, number and barcode of your loyalty cards (e.g. Payback) are stored only on your device, to show them at the checkout.
- **Camera (optional):** when you scan a loyalty card, Marqet reads the barcode on the device. No images are stored or transmitted.
- **Apple Intelligence (optional, Marqet Pro):** suggestions are computed by the on-device model; items do not leave your device.
- **Purchases** are handled entirely by Apple's App Store. We receive no payment data.

**Legal basis:** Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. Permissions can be revoked in iOS Settings at any time.

**Your rights:** We hold no personal data about you. Deleting the app deletes its data; your items remain in Apple Reminders. You may lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.
